# 24K Magic AI design direction

Night cruise deck, not a Tiny Desk gig poster. Write this down before changing `src/site/styles.css` and components.

- **Audience**: people who want themselves (or a few friends) dancing on a yacht deck in a gold, funk, “24K Magic” mood. They are not looking for an NPR studio, a band at a desk, or a generic prompt box.
- **Mood**: night sea, brass and champagne, a dance line you can cast. Quiet luxury, not a meme poster and not a tech demo.
- **Fonts**: headings Syne; body Outfit; times and prices IBM Plex Mono. Do not use Fraunces, Anton, Instrument Sans, or JetBrains Mono (those are Tiny Desk AI).
- **Colors**: night sea `#07111f`, panel `#121a2b`, foam text `#f4efe4`, champagne gold `#e7c56a` for buttons and the live dot, line `rgb(244 239 228 / 14%)`. No blue primary, no cream poster paper, no record-red `REC` tag, no amber stage light `#f2a33a`.
- **Home first screen**: one column. A short gold kicker, a large Syne title, one sentence, then the dance line (four cards: Lead, Port, Starboard, Rail). No stage photo of a real performance. The stand-in is an original deck drawing, labeled as a stand-in, until a licensed clip exists. Below the line: consent, the price, then the result. Further down: three steps, how this differs from a Tiny Desk video, pricing, FAQ.
- **Upload**: four upright cards in a row (scroll on a phone), not face pins on a collage. Tap a card to add a photo. A gold ring means that seat is filled. × clears it. Two presets: “Just me” and “Full line”.
- **Generating**: three lines of copy (casting, dancing, mixing) and a mono elapsed time. No fake percent.
- **Result**: one player, then two downloads (with the track, silent).
- **Content pages**: dark sheets, gold kickers, no halftone paper.
- **Avoid**: Tiny Desk’s left-stage / right-9:16 hero, dog cast, poster-paper sections, and the template’s light grey page with a blue button.
